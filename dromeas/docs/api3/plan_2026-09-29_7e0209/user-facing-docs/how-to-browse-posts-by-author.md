# How to browse posts by author

You can use the blog's author pages to see all published posts from a specific writer. This guide explains how to find an author and browse their posts.

## Prerequisites

- The site's blog has an **Authors** section.
- The author you want to read has at least one published post assigned to them.

## Browse posts by author

1. Open the site's **Blog**.
2. Select **Authors** to open the list of blog authors.
3. Find the author you want to read. Each author entry shows how many published posts they have.
4. Select the author's name.
5. Browse the list of posts shown on the author's page.
6. If the author has more than 10 posts, use the page navigation at the bottom to see older posts.

## Expected result

You see a list of published posts by the selected author, ordered with the most recent posts first. If the author has more than 10 published posts, the remaining posts appear on additional pages.

## Common issues

- **The Authors section is missing.** The site may not have any shared blog authors configured. Contact the site owner if you expect to see authors.
- **An author's page shows no posts.** The author may not have any published posts assigned to them yet, or their posts may be unpublished.
- **An author appears in the list but has no individual page.** That author may be listed for display purposes only, without a dedicated page for their posts.
- **The same person appears under multiple author names.** Author names are managed by the site. Contact the site owner to merge or correct author entries.
