# How to read blog posts

You can browse and read any published blog post from your site's **Blog** page. This guide covers finding posts, opening them, and moving between posts.

## Prerequisites

- Your site includes a blog with at least one published post
- You can reach the **Blog** page from the site navigation or homepage

## Read a blog post

1. Open your site and navigate to the **Blog** page.
2. Browse the blog list. Each entry shows the post title, date, author, short description, and estimated reading time. By default, the newest post appears first.
3. If the blog has many posts, use the page controls at the bottom of the list to move between list pages. The default list size is 10 posts per page.
4. Select the title of the post you want to read. The full post opens on its own page.
5. To read another recent post, use the **Recent posts** list on the side of the page.
6. To move between posts, use the previous post and next post navigation on the post page.

## Expected result

After you select a post title, the full article opens with its title, author, publication date, and complete content. You can read the entire post and use the navigation to continue to other posts.

## Common issues

- **A post does not appear in the blog list**: The post may still be a draft or may be unlisted, so it is not shown in the public blog listing. Check with the post author or site administrator.
- **The blog list is empty**: There may be no published posts yet, or the blog may not be enabled on the site. Contact your site administrator.
- **The blog list shows the full article instead of a short preview**: The post may be missing the break that separates a preview from the full article. Ask the post author to add a read more break.
